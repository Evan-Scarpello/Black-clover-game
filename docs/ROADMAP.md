## 2. Priority order for "Planned"

| Priority | Feature | Why now **(estimate)** |
|---|---|---|
| **P1** | **Mailbox** | Small build, and every other live-ops feature and the store launch depend on it (compensation, event rewards) |
| **P1** | **Events calendar and event shop** | Turns the existing modes into weekly variety without new systems. Feeds banner hype |
| **P1** | **Cross-device accounts** | Blocks the store launch. Unlocks safe PvP and clans at scale |
| **P1** | **Trial stage for banner heroes** | One BattleSpec per banner. Raises pull confidence |
| **P2** | **Gear and equipment** | The long-term sink the economy needs once players clear the campaign and tower. Big balance job, so ship after events |
| **P2** | **Friends and friendship summons** | Viral loop and a free pull source. Reuses arena snapshots for "borrow a hero" |
| **P2** | **Hero resonance / level sync** | Makes new Mythics instantly usable and raises gacha value. Must land before the roster passes ~60 heroes |
| **P2** | **Monthly card and skins** | Low-risk revenue that isn't gacha (helpful for BE/BR rules) |
| **P3** | **Element wheel** | Adds PvP counterplay, but means re-tagging and re-balancing every hero. Do it at a season boundary |
| **P3** | **Roguelite labyrinth** | Great variety, but needs relic content and UI. Engine supports it (persistent HP across BattleSpecs) |
| **P3** | **Seasonal content resets** | Only after the event cadence is proven |
| **P4** | **Guild wars, real-time PvP, VIP** | Need a large population and server-authoritative infrastructure. VIP is an optics risk |

## 4. What makes these games stick

- **Progress never stops.** An idle chest plus short, frequent sessions: open, claim, upgrade, leave in 2 minutes (AFK Arena, Idle Heroes).
- **Collection is the content.** Every new hero is a new toy. Bonds and faction synergies turn pulls into team-building goals, not just stats.
- **Fair gacha builds trust.** Hard pity, visible odds and a featured guarantee let players save with a plan. Limited banners create urgency without feeling like a scam.
- **Layered clocks.** Dailies, then weeklies (arena season, clan raid), then seasons and passes. There's always something about to reset, which is a reason to return at every timescale.
- **Social obligation.** Clan raids and donations make you show up for other people. This is the strongest D30+ retention force in the genre **(estimate)**.
- **Visible mastery.** Tower floors, stage stars, arena rank and a clan leaderboard. A number that goes up and a name next to it.
- **Real decisions in an auto-battler.** Formations, row traits and formation-shifting ultimates (our hook), plus counters such as elements later, mean thinking beats raw power, at least sometimes.
- **Steady live ops.** A new Mythic, event or mode every couple of weeks keeps the meta moving and gives lapsed players a reason to come back.
- **Respect the player.** Generous early game, clear odds, no dark patterns, refunds and compensation via mail. Regulators (FTC, Brazil, Korea) now enforce this, and players reward it.
